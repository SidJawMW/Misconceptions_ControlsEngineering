Chapter . Controller Summary

A parallel-form PI controller regulates the speed of one wheel motor to a commanded 10 rad/s. It runs discrete at 0.02 s, the rate of the embedded speed task, and limits its own output to +/-12 V with clamping anti-windup so that a stalled wheel cannot wind the integrator up against a voltage the driver cannot deliver. The gains were obtained with PID Tuner against a linearization of the loop, then validated by simulation against the requirements below.
